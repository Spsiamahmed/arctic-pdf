FAQ

Is Arctic PDF safe for confidential files? +

Yes, 100%. Unlike other online converters, we do not upload your files to a server. All processing happens inside your browser's memory and is destroyed as soon as you close the tab.

Does it reduce quality too much? +

You have total control. Our engine allows you to set the quality level. At 70%, you typically get a 50-70% reduction in size with almost no visible quality loss.
